# SerenPsycheion

**The mind's workshop for a Seren body.** [SerenSoma](https://github.com/ChadRoesler/SerenSoma)
is the body: a Stack-chan robot's firmware and the voice server that is its
nervous system. Psycheion is where you watch it and shape it.

- **Console:** what the body is doing now. Its face, battery and the light
  rails as they really are; what each touch was read as and which face it
  brought up; how close a scratch is to tipping the face over.
- **Settings:** the lights, speaker and screen (saved on the robot), night
  hours and their caps, work hours, overwhelm.
- **What it senses:** a plain statement of what the body's microphone,
  camera and touch do, what's kept and where it goes, for you and for anyone
  you hand a body to.
- **Faces:** Face Studio. Draw and pose a face, give it sounds and
  build-ups, send it to the robot, share it as one `.zip`.

It reaches the body only through [SerenSinew](https://github.com/ChadRoesler/SerenSinew)'s
`seren_sinew.soma`, and it holds the body's tokens itself: your browser only
ever holds Psycheion's own. There's no camera button, by design.

The package README under [`SerenPsycheion/`](SerenPsycheion/README.md) has
setup and configuration.
